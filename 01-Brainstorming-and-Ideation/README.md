# Phase 1 – Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Title
FitBuddy – AI Fitness Plan Generator using Gemini Models

## Problem Statement
Many people find it difficult to organize a simple fitness routine according to their goals, activity level, available time, and preferences. They may not know how to structure their weekly activities or where to begin.

## Proposed Solution
FitBuddy is an AI-powered application that uses Gemini Models to generate a structured general fitness plan based on information provided by the user.

## Objectives
- To develop an easy-to-use AI fitness planning application.
- To collect basic fitness goals and preferences from users.
- To use Gemini Models to generate structured fitness suggestions.
- To provide the generated plan in a simple and understandable format.
- To demonstrate the practical use of generative AI.

## Target Users
- Beginners interested in general fitness planning.
- Users who want help organizing their workout routine.
- Students and individuals interested in AI-assisted fitness planning.

## Key Features
- Simple user interface.
- Fitness goal selection.
- Activity-level input.
- Workout preference input.
- AI-generated fitness plan.
- Structured and readable output.
- Gemini Model integration.

## Brainstorming Ideas
During brainstorming, the following ideas were considered:
1. AI fitness plan generator.
2. AI meal suggestion application.
3. AI study planner.
4. AI budget assistant.
5. AI workout recommendation system.

## Selected Idea
The AI fitness plan generator was selected because it combines generative AI with a practical everyday application and demonstrates how Gemini Models can generate useful structured content from user input.

## Expected Outcome
The expected outcome is a working FitBuddy application that accepts user information and generates a structured general fitness plan using Gemini Models.

## Safety Note
FitBuddy is intended for general informational and planning purposes. AI-generated suggestions should not be treated as medical advice or as a replacement for advice from qualified health or fitness professionals.
